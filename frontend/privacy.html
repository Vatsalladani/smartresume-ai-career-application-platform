1. Information We Collect

We collect information you explicitly provide: account details (name, email address), career profile data (work history, skills, education), and evidence links. We do not scrape unauthorized third-party accounts.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your career data is used solely to provide resume generation, job fit analysis, and interview preparation services to you. We do not sell your personal data to recruiters, advertisers, or third parties.

3. AI Processing & Zero Training Policy

Your private resume text and interview answers are processed via secure, enterprise-grade AI APIs. Your private candidate data is never used to train public generative AI models.

4. Media Privacy Guarantee

Video streams used in the Live AI Interview module are processed locally in your browser memory for preview purposes only. Video is never recorded, stored, or analyzed for biometric markers.

5. Account & Data Deletion

You may export or permanently delete your account and all associated career records at any time directly from the Settings workspace.
